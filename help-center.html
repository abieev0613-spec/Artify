Payment issues

Checkout is a demo flow, not a real payment gateway.

  • If checkout does not continue, check that your delivery address is filled in.
  • For UPI, card or net-banking demo methods, enter a placeholder reference only — never enter real card details.
  • If the page says it cannot connect, run the Node.js server and open Artify through localhost:3000.
  • If an order appears after checkout, the demo payment step was recorded as successful.
📦

Orders & delivery

  • Open My Orders to see orders linked to your account.
  • Use Track Order and enter the order ID shown in your order history.
  • If the order is missing, sign in with the same email/mobile account used at checkout.
  • Tracking milestones are illustrative in this demo; they are not live courier updates.
↩️

Cancellation issues

  • Open your order history and use the cancel option for an eligible confirmed order.
  • Cancelled orders remain in your history so you can review what happened.
  • Since payments are simulated, the demo does not issue a real refund.
  • If a cancellation button is unavailable, the order may already be cancelled or no longer eligible.
🔐

Login & account

  • You can log in using the email address or mobile number associated with your account.
  • Make sure the password is the one used when registering.
  • This demo does not send OTPs or password-reset messages.
  • Keep your password private and do not reuse important account passwords.
♡

Cart & wishlist

  • Use the heart on a product to add or remove it from your wishlist.
  • Cart and wishlist changes show a message and are saved in this browser.
  • If the count looks old, refresh the page. Private browsing may not preserve saved items.
  • Make sure the item is still available in the product collection.
🎨

Product & custom art

  • Search by product name or category and refine results using filters.
  • Switch between grid and list views to browse your way.
  • For custom art, describe the idea, style, preferred budget and useful details.
  • Product pictures and prices in this demo are sample catalogue data.
QUICK ANSWERS

Still wondering?

Why does my payment say demo or sample?

Artify records a simulated payment method and reference; it does not connect to a bank or payment provider.

Can I use Artify from another device?

Accounts and orders are saved in the server-side demo database, but cart, wishlist and theme preferences are stored in the browser. This is not a production multi-device account system.

How do I report a bug?

Visit Contact and include the page name, what you clicked, what you expected and what happened. Never send your password or real payment information.
