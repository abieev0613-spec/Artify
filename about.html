WHO WE ARE

A marketplace with a human heartbeat.

Artify is imagined as a welcoming online studio for handmade art and crafts. Instead of treating products as anonymous items on a shelf, we celebrate the imagination, time and care that go into each kind of creation. Our collection spans paintings, crochet, jewellery, bouquets, crafts and home décor — giving shoppers a place to find pieces for their homes, their celebrations and the people they love.

🌱

🎁

🪡

Creative variety

OUR MISSION ♡

Make meaningful things easier to find.

Our mission is to make handmade shopping feel both inspiring and straightforward. Visitors can explore products, search by name or category, use price and rating filters, switch between grid and list views, save favourites, manage a cart, request custom art and follow their order history.

We want the digital experience to feel considered too: an accessible layout, smooth navigation, readable product information, a global light/dark theme, helpful support pages and a clear sample checkout journey.

Artify is a demo project, so its checkout simulates a payment result and does not charge real money. The experience is designed to demonstrate how the customer journey can work from discovery through order tracking.
